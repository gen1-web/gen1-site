My Salah Prayer times · Adhan · Qibla

Everything you need to pray on time.

Built for daily use by people who pray five times a day, with the details that matter: your mosque's jamaat, a full adhan you can stop, and reminders that fit your routine.

Accurate prayer times

Calculated on your phone for your location, with Hanafi and standard Asr and 12 calculation methods to match your mosque.

Full adhan at prayer time

Choose any sound on your phone or your own adhan recording. It plays in full, and a Stop button ends it.

Never miss jamaat

Add your local mosques and their iqamah times, including Jumuah. Get reminded before iqamah, with a countdown to jamaat.

Reminders your way

Adhan time, before prayer, before jamaat, plus Shurooq, Ishraq, Duha, Zawal, Islamic midnight and Tahajjud.

Home screen widgets

Widgets in many sizes and your colours: next prayer, today's times, adhan and iqamah, your mosque's next jamaat and a tap-to-count tasbih. They turn red when jamaat is less than 15 minutes away.

Fajr alarm

A real alarm for Fajr that rings even on silent and only stops when you solve a simple sum, so you are properly awake.

Qibla compass

Find the Qibla wherever you are. It tells you which way to turn and shows the distance to Makkah.

Tasbih and daily adhkar

Tap anywhere to count with your own dhikr, and read the morning and evening adhkar.

Hijri and Gregorian calendar

Both calendars side by side, with Islamic days marked.

Prayer tracker

Log your prayers and see daily, weekly and monthly stats, with streaks and awards.

Ramadan and Quran plan

A Suhoor countdown on your Today screen, and a daily page target to help you finish the Quran this Ramadan.

Private prayer break

Pause reminders for the days you cannot pray and keep your streak safe. It is private and saved only on your phone.

Verses for your day

A reminder card with verses from the Quran and their translation. Swipe or tap for the next one.

Six languages

English, Arabic, Bahasa Indonesia, Bangla, Hindi and Urdu.

16 colour themes

Pick from 16 colour themes, including Follow the sky.

Backup and restore

Back up your settings and data, and restore them whenever you need.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Private by design: your information stays on your phone.

Walletly

Expense Tracker

A bold, private money tracker for Android, built for everyday life in Pakistan.

Record what you earn and spend in a few taps, by voice, or by scanning a receipt. See exactly where your money goes, set budgets and savings goals, track your net worth, and keep everything on your own phone. No sign-up and no account to create. Most expense apps are either too complicated to keep up, or want your financial life on a server. Walletly does one job well: you log a transaction in seconds, and it turns into a clear picture of your month. PKR is the default currency, and Pakistani banks and mobile wallets are built in.

Log it. See it. Keep it private.

Everything Walletly does, and nothing it doesn't: a fast way to record your money, a clear view of where it goes, and your data staying on your own phone.

Log spending in seconds

A large amount field with its own number keypad, so you never fight the phone keyboard. Pick expense or income, choose a category and an account, and save. Home updates straight away.

Type it, say it, scan it

Say or type something like “gas 40 from Chase yesterday” and Walletly fills in the amount, category, account and date for you to check and save. You can also scan a receipt, or use the built-in calculator keypad.

Transfers and favourites

Move money between your accounts, and log your usual purchases, like Coffee, Lunch or Gas, with a single tap.

Your month at a glance

Home shows your balance for the month, with income and spending shown separately, plus your latest transactions. Income gets a plus sign and spending a minus sign, so you can read it without relying on colour.

Full history

Move month by month, filter by All, Expense or Income, and see every day's entries grouped with a daily total.

Insights

Total spending for the month, a category-by-category breakdown, and a six-month comparison so you can tell if this month is higher or lower than usual.

Monthly budget

Set a monthly budget and see what is left, how much you can spend each day, and whether you are on track for month end.

Savings goals

Progress bars for every goal, plus the monthly amount you need to reach it by your date. See how much you saved each month and as a share of your income.

Net worth

One number for everything you have: accounts, loans, gold and other assets, in any currency, with a chart of how it has grown over time.

Loans

Keep track of money you have lent. What is owed to you counts toward your net worth.

Gold, silver and assets

Value your gold and silver in grams, ounces or tola at daily international rates, or enter your own local jeweller's rate. Add other assets, like a car, with a value you enter.

Every account in one place

Cash is ready from the start. Add accounts from a searchable list of Pakistani banks, mobile wallets and international services, each with its own opening balance and an optional last 4 digits to tell cards apart.

Work in your currency

PKR by default, with USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR and more. Foreign-currency accounts show the converted amount in your main currency. Rates update daily and are saved on your phone, so it works offline, and each transaction remembers the rate it was entered at.

Daily reminders

Pick a time and get a daily nudge to log your expenses.

Make it yours

Six accent colours, your name on the home screen, and smooth, subtle animations when you save, in the Poppins typeface on a dark design that is easy on the eyes.

Home screen widgets

Add an expense, income or voice entry in one tap from your home screen, and see what is left, what you spent today and your savings goals at a glance.

App lock

Protect the app with a PIN, with fingerprint or face unlock on top. The PIN is stored in scrambled form on your device, and repeated wrong tries trigger increasing waiting times.

Hide amounts

One tap on the eye icon masks every amount, handy in public. The app remembers your choice.

Backup and restore

Export everything to a single file and save it to Google Drive, send it to yourself or keep it anywhere. Restore checks the file, shows what it contains and asks you to confirm before replacing anything. Backups from older versions still work.

Last 4 digits only

The app never stores full card or account numbers. It keeps only the last 4 digits and cuts anything longer before saving.

Your data stays on your phone.

No accounts and no sign-in. The app fetches only currency exchange rates and gold and silver prices, and no personal or financial data is sent when it does.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start logging.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. It is stored on your phone. The app fetches only currency exchange rates and gold and silver prices.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Export a backup regularly and save it to Google Drive or send it to yourself. You can restore it on a new phone.

Does it use real bank logos?

No. Accounts show a coloured tile with the bank's initials.
